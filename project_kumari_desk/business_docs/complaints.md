# Kumari Catering · Complaints and Quality Policy

Any problem with the food (cold packs, short counts, wrong items, quality concerns) must be reported within 2 hours of delivery, by phone or WhatsApp with a photo where possible.

Complaints reported within the window receive a same-day replacement or a full refund of the affected packs, customer's choice, no questions asked.

Complaints reported after 2 hours cannot be accepted: food held out of refrigeration is outside the kitchen's control.

Serious quality complaints are logged, and the affected batch's supplier is reviewed. Repeat issues with a supplier end the relationship.
