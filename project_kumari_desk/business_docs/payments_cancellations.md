# Kumari Catering · Payments, Deposits and Cancellations

Payment is by cash or bank transfer only. Card payments are not accepted.

Orders above Rs. 30,000 require a 50% deposit at confirmation. The balance is due on delivery.

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the delivery time receive the full deposit back. Cancellations inside 48 hours forfeit the deposit: ingredients are already bought.

Date changes requested at least 48 hours ahead are free, subject to kitchen availability.
