# Kumari Catering · Delivery

Delivery is free within 5 km of Maharagama junction.

Beyond 5 km, delivery is a flat Rs. 1,000, covering Colombo and the suburbs. Outside greater Colombo, delivery is not available; pickup from the kitchen is welcome.

There are no deliveries on poya days. Poya-day orders are delivered the evening before, packed for overnight refrigeration with reheating instructions.

Delivery times are agreed when the order is confirmed. The driver calls the number on the order 30 minutes before arriving.
