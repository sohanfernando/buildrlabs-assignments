# Kumari Catering · Equipment Rental

Chafing dishes rent at Rs. 800 per day, the large 10 kg rice cooker at Rs. 1,200 per day, and the 40-cup tea urn at Rs. 600 per day.

Rental equipment is available only alongside a catering order, not separately.

Equipment is delivered and collected with the food. The renter pays for damage or loss at replacement cost, stated on the rental note at handover.
