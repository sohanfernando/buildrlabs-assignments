# Kumari Catering · Menu and Pack Prices

Lunch packs, rice and curry: vegetarian Rs. 550, chicken Rs. 650, fish Rs. 700, seafood Rs. 850. Every lunch pack includes rice, three vegetable curries, sambol and papadam.

Dinner packs: string hoppers (15 pieces) with two curries and katta sambol, Rs. 600. Fried rice pack with chicken and chilli paste, Rs. 750.

Short eats trays: 50 pieces Rs. 4,500, 100 pieces Rs. 8,500. A tray mixes fish buns, cutlets, patties and rolls; an all-vegetarian tray is available at the same price.

Morning functions: milk rice with lunu miris and jaggery, Rs. 350 per person, minimum 25 people.

Full-service buffets (weddings, almsgivings) are priced per event after a tasting; typical range Rs. 1,400 to Rs. 2,200 per guest depending on the menu.
