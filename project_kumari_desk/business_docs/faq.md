# Kumari Catering · Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours? The kitchen answers 7 am to 8 pm every day except poya days, when it is closed.

Do you do vegetarian or halal menus? Fully vegetarian menus, yes, any size. Halal chicken is available on request with 3 days notice; it comes from a certified supplier and adds Rs. 50 per pack.

Can we taste before a big event? Tastings are included for full-service events and available for large pack orders (100+) at Rs. 2,500, refunded against the order.

Do you provide serving staff? Only for full-service buffet events. Pack deliveries do not include staff.

Is there a discount for very large orders? Orders of 200 packs and above get 5% off the pack price. This does not combine with other offers.

Who do I contact? 011 285 4770, call or WhatsApp. Slow replies at lunchtime: everyone is cooking.
