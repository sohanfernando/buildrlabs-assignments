# Kumari Catering · Ordering Rules

The minimum order is 20 packs. Orders below 20 packs are not accepted; the burners do not fire for less.

Standard orders need 2 days notice. Orders of 100 packs or more need 4 days notice.

Full-service events (buffet, serving staff, warmers) need 2 weeks notice and include a tasting session at the kitchen, booked in advance.

Orders falling on a poya day are prepared and delivered the evening before: the kitchen is closed on poya days.

To place an order, message or call 011 285 4770 (WhatsApp on the same number) with the date, the pack counts, and the delivery address.
